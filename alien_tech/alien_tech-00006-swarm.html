The Swarm Problem

Coordinating 100 drones usually requires a central supercomputer calculating 10,000 trajectories to avoid collisions. It's slow, heavy, and fails if the wifi drops.

The Bangsaen Solution: Fluid Dynamics

We don't treat drones as individual units. We treat the entire swarm as a single Incompressible Fluid using Koopman Operators.

  • Flow State: Each drone is just a particle flowing in a mathematical river.
  • Zero Collisions: Water molecules don't crash into each other; they slide past. Our drones do the same.
  • Infinite Scalability: 10 drones or 10,000 drones... the math equation remains the same size.
